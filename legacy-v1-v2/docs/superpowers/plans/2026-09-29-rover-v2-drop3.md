# Rover v2 — Drop 3 (electronics, tower, body, checks, documents) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use superpowers:executing-plans (inline). Steps use checkbox syntax.

**Goal:** Finish the machine (electronics tray, battery, sensor tower, hood, covers, handle, arrows, ToF noses), enforce the rules, balance and mass checks, lighten to the limit, and deliver renders, viewer, build guide, BOM, order list and change log.

**Architecture:** As drops 1–2. New modules build items into the same `Assembly`; `checks/rules.py` adds A8/A9; `checks/runner.py` turns on the mass and balance limits.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-29-rover-v2-design.md` §7.4–7.6, §8, §9, §11. Drop plans: `…-drop1.md`, `…-drop2.md`.

## Global Constraints
Same as drops 1–2 (bed 210 × 210 × 240, 180 g cap, overhang rule with bridges ≤ 8 mm, collisions ≤ 1 mm³, no drilling or inserts, colours by function, nothing pushed or published without the user's OK), plus:
- **Decided while modelling:** the tray halves are 3 mm plates with hanging ribs resting on the rails; the tie plate lies on top of both halves behind the lift motor; the hood is three panels printed upside down (1.6 mm walls); the tower is stacked collars that slide onto a 2020 post (each clamped by two M5 screws); arrows are three separate 0.8 mm plates taped on (a recess in a roof cannot print without bridging); ToF sensors sit on noses that bolt to the pivot-bracket walls; camera view rule: at pan 0° only the excavator may appear in the cone, at pan 180° nothing may.
- Mass: hard limit 8.0 kg empty, goal 7.5 kg; loaded front-axle share ≤ 75 %.

## Tasks
1. **Electronics parts and tray** (`lib/cots.py`, `modules/electronics.py`): boards as boxes from listings (Pi 5 85×56×17 with cooler, ESP32 51×28×12, BTS7960 ×4 50×50×16, buck 45×21×14, relay 30×28×28, fuse holders, battery pack 70×60×40, MPU6050); `tray_left`, `tray_right`, `tray_tie`, `batt_cradle`. Test: no collisions, tray parts pass `check_oriented`, boards sit on standoffs, gap ≥ 1 mm to the lift motor and cradle.
2. **Tower** (`modules/tower.py`): 300 mm post, `tower_base`, `tower_sleeve` ×2, `tower_estop`, `tower_pzem`, `tower_head` with SG90 and webcam, rear ToF. Test: parts printable, collar clamps take the post, E-stop head Ø60 at a side shelf, PZEM window faces −x.
3. **Body** (`modules/body.py`): `hood_left/right/spine`, `body_side_cover` ×2, `body_handle`, `body_arrow` ×3, `tof_nose` ×2. Test: hood panels ≤ 200 mm, no collisions at any pose, handle clears the link.
4. **Rules and balance** (`checks/rules.py`, `checks/runner.py`): A8 stowed size at pose 35, E-stop ≥ 40 mm with clear approach, PZEM display normal within ±30° of rearward and centre ≥ 250 mm high, three arrow plates on upward faces; A9 sensor cones; A6 mass ≤ 8 000 g, A7 loaded front share ≤ 75 %.
5. **Lightweighting pass** until the mass check passes: drum plates and rings, drive mounts, wheels, arm boss, hood walls.
6. **Documents and finish:** viewer and final renders (six fixed views, dig pose, exploded), `docs/v2-build-guide.md`, `hardware/bom_v2.csv`, `docs/v2-order-list.md`, `docs/v2-changelog.md`, `CONTEXT.md` update, spec sync, `git tag v2-drop3`.
